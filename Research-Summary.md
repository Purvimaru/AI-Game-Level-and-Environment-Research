AI is making great advances in the field of video game design
and is rapidly changing the PCG approach. This paper focuses
on AI and its methods such as neural networks and
reinforcement learning to look into the best approaches for
creating game level, increasing personalization and increasing
player experience. Through Unity Muse, OpenAI’s GPT-4, and
Experience-Driven Reinforcement Learning (EDRL), AI can
build levels on the fly as well as parse the degree of difficulty
to which the majority of players can be challenged. However,
even with such developments noticeable barriers and
drawbacks continue to persist, including algorithm intricacy,
and personalization constraints. This research is related to
how games can be built more efficiently using AI and how AI
can help reduce technostress while making story-adaptive
games more engaging. Analyzing the state-of-the-art in game
AI, this paper also explores how the variety of current
applications in such game as Super Mario Bros., Minecraft,
and No Man’s Sky, shows possibilities of striking a right
balance between algorithmic outsourcing of content
generation and embracing creativity.
