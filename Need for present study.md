Various trends in modern video games raise issues related to time and costs as well as content creation issues of increasingly complex video games. Technostress for oftener times tired or burnt out game designers is then negated in parallel: with artificial intelligence tools learned to generate content on their own.[6] Also, AI in gaming delivers a vast quantity and great variety of changing and complex scenarios, which can be useful for testing and improving algorithms in real-world contexts.[2]
The following research questions shall therefore seek to establish how AI technologies can be applied to making improvements to game level designs/creation and further, the ultimate player experiences.[9] It will also examine how AI can enhance self-telling and dynamic storying to similarly enhance client involvement and pleasure.[8]
